Burrow Bound · Android Game

Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you play Burrow Bound.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Who we are

Burrow Bound (the “Game”) is published by Blackstone Interactive and developed by Bryan Powell (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy describes how information is stored, collected, used, and shared in connection with the Android version of the Game. For privacy inquiries, contact us at bryan.powell370@gmail.com.

2. Game progress stored on your device

Burrow Bound stores gameplay progress, unlocked content, game settings, and similar save data locally on your device so the Game can function and remember your progress. Local save data is generally removed when you clear the Game’s storage or uninstall it, subject to your device’s own storage and backup behavior. We do not require you to enter your name, phone number, or email address to play the core offline game.

3. Analytics and diagnostics

Where enabled and permitted by your choices, Burrow Bound may use Google Firebase services, including Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, to understand game performance and investigate errors. Information processed by these services can include:

  • Game events, such as level starts and completions, training actions, and interaction with features;
  • App version, device type, operating system version, session information, and approximate geographic information derived from technical signals;
  • App-instance or installation identifiers and, where available and permitted, advertising identifiers; and
  • Crash reports, error details, and performance or diagnostic information.

The Game is designed to offer an analytics/diagnostics choice in supported builds. Where that choice is available, you can review or change it in the Game’s Settings. Changing your choice can affect future collection; information already transmitted may remain subject to the provider’s retention and deletion procedures.

Firebase Remote Config, if enabled, may retrieve game configuration over the internet and process installation or device-related technical information needed to provide that service.

4. Optional rewarded advertisements

Some versions of Burrow Bound may offer optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. If this feature is active and you use it, Google and its advertising partners may process data needed to serve and measure advertisements, support security, and prevent fraud. Depending on the configuration and applicable permissions, this can include IP address (which may indicate approximate location), ad interactions and video views, device or advertising identifiers, and app diagnostic information.

Where required by law, the Game will provide advertising consent or privacy-choice options. If available in your version, look for Ad Privacy in the Game’s Settings. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily eliminate all data processing required to provide non-personalized ads or maintain security.

5. How information is used and shared

Information handled through active services may be used to operate and improve the Game, measure gameplay and advertising, investigate failures, manage game configuration, and protect services from fraud or abuse. We may share relevant information with service providers that perform those functions, primarily Google and its applicable advertising partners when their services are active. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. We do not claim that all in-app information remains solely on your device when online SDKs are enabled.

To learn more about Google’s processing practices, visit Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy Information.

6. Information security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved. Supported Google SDKs use encrypted network connections for data in transit. Local game data remains subject to the security of your device and operating system. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Retention and deletion

Local save data is kept on your device until it is removed through the Game or your device’s app storage controls, or through uninstalling the Game. Information sent to third-party services may be kept according to those services’ settings, retention periods, and legal obligations. You may contact us using the email below to request information about, or deletion of, data under our control. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law. Some technical information may not be directly identifiable to a particular player.

8. Children and families

Burrow Bound does not require players to enter personal contact details to play the core game. If you are a parent or guardian with concerns about a child’s data, please contact us. Advertising, analytics, and data handling for children must follow the audience settings and protections applicable to the version of the Game made available on Google Play.

9. Your choices

You can review applicable in-game privacy controls (where available), adjust Android advertising privacy settings, clear local app storage, or uninstall the Game. For a privacy request, use the contact below. Some options depend on which services are active in your installed build and on local law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy as features, providers, or legal requirements change. When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
